# Architecture and track seams

Next.js App Router, hosted on Vercel. Every page is statically generated at build time (`generateStaticParams`, `dynamicParams = false`, no runtime data), so Vercel serves it as static HTML. URLs have no trailing slash (Next's default); canonicals, the sitemap, `/llms.txt` and internal links all follow that form. Images go through `next/image`. The spec (`design-spec.md`) is the source of truth for look and motion. This file says who owns which files.

## Panels without intercepting routes

This build replaces the `app/@panel/(.)work/[slug]` plan in spec §2 with the steps below. The client chose to keep this pattern after the move to Vercel, where intercepting routes would also work.

1. The root layout (`src/app/layout.tsx`) always renders `<SkyHost/>` and `<Journey/>` behind `{children}`.
2. Every entity is a plain static route built with `generateStaticParams` and `dynamicParams = false`. That covers `/work/[slug]`, `/projects/[slug]`, `/about`, `/visual-arts/analog/[slug]`, `/visual-arts/digital/[slug]`.
3. A star link (`<SkyLink>`) records the target path in a small client store (`softNav`) before the soft navigation.
4. `<PanelFrame>` wraps each entity page. Server HTML is always the full page (the same centred column on the calm sky), so no-JS visits and crawlers read it. With JS it becomes the panel at hydration (the full page stays hidden until then, `@media (scripting: enabled)`, with a 3 s fallback), so every entity route on screen is a panel, never text over the live chart. There is one panel layout for every way in (R5): the camera flies to the entity's star and puts it 84 px (`--star-gap`) above the kicker (PanelFrame measures the kicker and calls `cameraRig.setFocalY`; `panelPose`), the sky dims and drops its labels, names, pole mark, lines and other content stars (`resolved.bare`), and the content reads in a centred column (680 px) below the star. A panel whose star and content fit the viewport (`data-fit`, measured on every resize) is one vertically centred unit with no scroll; a longer one (Meta, films with Up next) keeps the top-aligned scroller with the kicker at 30 vh. A painting, its caption and the step row fit together (the image is capped at `100svh − 344px`). Paintings (`variant="media"`) and films (`cinema`) lead with the work, centred and sized to the viewport, with title and caption or credits below. The column's scroller fades out at the top (mask) so text never scrolls over the star. Mobile is the same full-screen view at the page margins. Every panel has Close ✕ (44 px, top right), a "← Back to sky" link (top left) and Esc; prev/next (← →) sit in a quiet footer row. The panel is a non-modal `<dialog>` (`show()`), so the site header stays usable above it. If the current path equals the recorded path, the panel was opened from the sky and Close goes back in history. Otherwise (direct load, reload, a plain link) it is a direct panel: it fades in (T20) and Close fades it out and pushes the parent page: `/visual-arts/analog` or `/visual-arts/digital` for paintings and films, else `/#chart` (the H3 rest under full motion; the top of the home page under reduced motion, which has no chart stop). Steps from a direct panel stay direct. Header links dismiss an open panel instantly (`softNav.dismiss()`, set by `Journey` on a header click): no exit animation, one move to the destination.

URLs and SEO are the same in both modes.

## Content

`src/content/*.ts` is the single source of truth: `site.ts` (person, socials, résumé, tech, education; Georgia Tech is `inProgress`, so it is an `affiliation` in JSON-LD, never `alumniOf`), `work.ts`, `projects.ts` (one featured build, Section-8-Scout, plus `retiredProjects` for the redirects), `paintings.ts`, `films.ts`, and `sky.ts`. `sky.ts` is the chart as plain data: each constellation lists its stars' normalised positions, unlabelled `helpers` (dimmer stars that complete a figure), `lines` (which may use helper ids) and an optional `lodestar` (drawn brighter with a four-point glint). Reshaping a constellation is a data-only edit there; the entity modules carry only the spectral class. Portrait placement of each group is in `chart.ts` (`PORTRAIT_GROUPS`, `PORTRAIT_NAMES`). Pages, sitemap, JSON-LD and `/llms.txt` all read from these files. Copy is verbatim from `assets/content-reference.md`. Film credits keep the line breaks from the old `src/data`, which content-reference.md flattened.

| Journey | Scroll | `src/components/journey/*`, the `#journey` block of `src/app/page.tsx` | `Journey` (layout) owns Lenis (`getLenis()`); `HomeJourney` (home page) builds the ScrollTriggers, only inside `gsap.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)')`, and sets `<html data-journey="pinned\|mobile">`. Without it, `JourneySections` is the static R3 · RM layout. Each segment maps scroll to its own 0–1 progress (pinned stages: 0 = first dwell, 1 = last dwell, so stop i of n is at i / (n − 1); a one-dwell stage is always 0); one GSAP-ticker callback picks the segment the scroll is in and calls `cameraRig.setSegment`. Work has six stops (five stars, then "complete"; on mobile also The Student), then one featured-build stop (Paper R4 · P · Featured). `stepper.ts` makes one gesture one stop across those stops: wheel, trackpad, touch and ↑ ↓ PageUp PageDown Space each move exactly one stop (800 ms) and the rest of the gesture is swallowed (a wheel gesture ends after a 180 ms gap, or a 2.5× rise once the flight has landed); at the first and last stop the next gesture is let through to native scroll; free scroll (inertia from the hero, a scrollbar drag) is caught at the first stop it would cross, and a rest between stops settles on the nearer one. Every programmatic scroll (steps, rail ticks, the hero snap, hash landings) goes through `goTo`, which the stepper treats as busy. `/#work`, `/#projects`, `/#chart` land on Brava, the featured build and the H3 rest with an immediate jump (the camera flies there): synchronously after the build, re-landed for 2 s if a refresh moves the stop. No CSS scroll-snap. The journey never calls `setTarget`. A pinned stage carries `data-live` while its pin is active; its rail is hidden otherwise, so Tab from the chart never lands on a passed rail and scrolls back. It draws CSS stand-ins for the hero mask and sky and the pole mark for visitors without JS only (`@media (scripting: enabled)` hides them from the first paint; swapping them out mid-intro was a visible flash), and focal-star stand-ins hidden by `html[data-sky="live"]`. It also publishes `journeyProgress`. |
| Panels | Panels | `src/components/panels/*` | `PanelFrame({ slug, title, kicker, lead, subline, prev, next, counter, variant })` (`text`, `media`, `cinema`), `StepLink`, `SkyLink`, `softNav` (`set`, `close`, `dismiss`/`takeDismiss`, `closingUntil`), `useArrivedFromSky(pathname)` (read once per mount), `FilmView`, `VisualArtsToggle`, `ArtWindow`, and `transitions.css` (the global view-transition rules). Panel mode is a non-modal `<dialog>`; Esc and ← → are handled on the document while it is open. Close, Back to sky, Esc and a click on the empty sky around the column fade the panel out (240 ms, 12 px sink) and then go back in history (or push the parent page), while the camera flies back. Browser Back can't be animated before it commits, so a detached copy fades instead. After either, the first visible `SkyLink` for the panel's path takes focus once the fade has finished (sky markers, `tabIndex=-1`, never take it; an off-screen link takes focus without scrolling the page). Prev/next replace history in panel mode and push on a full page. View-transition names: `star-panel` (the dialog; rises 12 px and fades in on a soft open), `va-toggle`, `va-title`, `va-works`, `film-still-<slug>`. ## Hosting

- Old CRA URLs (`/analog`, `/analog/<old_id>`, `/digital`, `/digital/<old_id>`) get permanent (308) redirects to the new slugs. They are defined in `next.config.ts` `redirects()` and built from the `legacyId` fields in `src/content`.
- `/visual-arts` answers 308 → `/visual-arts/analog` (the hub page is gone). The retired projects (`/projects/freecast`, `react-dynamic-image`, `concord`, `react-2048`, `brickbreaker`) answer 308 → `/projects/section-8-scout`. Both are in `next.config.ts`.
- No `vercel.json`. Creating and linking the Vercel project, the first deploy, and DNS wait for the client's approval. The AWS pipeline (`buildspec.yml`, S3, CloudFront) is retired.

## No-JS and reduced motion

The HTML mirror is the page. Every word of content is in the server HTML. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `Journey` builds nothing (native scroll) and CSS transitions collapse. The sky track also has to follow the reduced-motion rules in spec §7.

In full motion on `/`, once the sky draws, the home mirror (`.main` in `page.tsx`: Work and featured-build index (desktop pinned journey only, where the journey stops are hidden after their pin), Observer, Painter, Director) is visually hidden because the chart presents the same stars. Screen readers still read it, and it comes back on screen when it holds a `:focus-visible` element (a keyboard user tabbing in, or a keyboard close returning focus). After it, `ChartExplore` (zoom ± and the T18 keys) closes the tab order. The site footer is fixed there too, so the document ends at the H3 rest: its links sit in the H3 HUD (top right under the header on desktop, a bottom row under the zoom controls on mobile), shown at H3 (`html[data-explore]`) or while they hold focus. Everywhere else, and under reduced motion or without JS, it is the normal footer. `#main` passes pointer events through to the sky's star markers except on text and controls. Under reduced motion or without JS, the mirror stays fully visible, and below the hero the sky is quiet (no labels) and dimmed.

## Integration notes

- Bundles: three and R3F load lazily. `SkyCanvas.tsx` (Canvas + `Scene`) is imported with `next/dynamic`, and the pose resolver that needs three lives in `resolve.ts`. `chart.ts` and `StarLabels.tsx` must not import `three`, or the ~950 KB chunk goes back into every page.
- Header: the RSF mark (home link) and Work · Projects · Visual Arts · About. One RSF mark on screen at a time: the header mark hides while the hero mask shows (`data-chrome` not `full`), while the H3 pole mark shows (`html[data-pole]`) and while the Visual Arts window shows (`html[data-window]`). On `/` the header shows only the centred menu at first; `HomeJourney` sets `<html data-chrome="hero|fly|full">` from the hero progress. The menu fades while the mask flies in, and the full header slides in from 60 % of the hero pin.
- Footer: socials and Download Résumé only (no mark, no Visual Arts link). `body` is a flex column at least the viewport tall and `main` grows, so on a short page the footer sits at the bottom of the viewport.
- Intro (T1, `HeroMark.tsx`): one GSAP timeline, 1.9 s. R, S, F draw in reading order with overlapping eased strokes, the fill swells under the F and then cross-fades with the sky. Scroll, keys or a tap ramp the timeline's `timeScale` to 2.5 (never a jump). The H1 labels are covered by the letterbox above them; they carry no CSS mask (a mask resized every scroll frame re-rasterised the layer and flashed). On desktop a label shows only if its anchor and both ends of its text fall inside the letters: `syncLabels` tests them against the `rsf-mark.svg` path (`Path2D`, `isPointInPath`) at the letterbox's current transform and fades the rest with the letterbox (per-element opacity). Labels stay hidden until the first frame has placed them.
- Visual Arts entry (`ArtWindow.tsx`, Paper B1): `/visual-arts/analog` and `/visual-arts/digital` open on the RSF letterforms as a window over three works: three layers over the mark's box, each masked by its own letter (`rsf-r.svg`, `rsf-s.svg`, `rsf-f.svg`, cut from `rsf.svg` with the same viewBox), so no work bleeds into a neighbouring letter. The three are a random draw per page load from the page's pool (every painting; every film with a still), with captions to match. The server HTML shows a fixed set (Dr. Manhattan, Marilyn Monroe, Walter White; Nightshade, Spare Key, Bayonet); with JS the stage stays hidden until the client's draw has loaded, so nothing swaps on screen. In full motion with JS it is a fixed layer that waits for the visitor: scrolling, ↓ / Space / Enter, a swipe or the "Scroll to enter" button flies through the S (scale 7, fade, 1 s) into the constellation and lands at its top (scroll 0). The entering gesture is consumed until input has been quiet for 200 ms and any touch has ended (trackpad inertia, a held swipe), so none of it scrolls the constellation; after that toggling segments goes straight to the works for the rest of the page load. The window has no Analog | Digital toggle; the constellation views do. Reduced motion and no JS: the same window, static, above the constellation; with JS, ↓ at the top or the button scrolls the constellation's header to where the window began. On the constellation views a selected work's logline and "Enter to open" are overlays (no reflow), and the others dim with `filter: brightness(0.5)`, never opacity, so the sky never shows through a work.
- Attribute names: the journey selects `#journey [data-stage]`. The sky's label root uses `data-section`, `data-level`, `data-mask` and `data-overview`. Don't reuse journey attribute names on sky elements: GSAP once pinned the sky's label layer this way.
- Vimeo: the client's plan is Basic, which ignores `controls: false`, so `VIMEO_NATIVE_CONTROLS` in `src/components/panels/FilmView.tsx` is `true`. Our poster and play button show until the first play; after that Vimeo's controls are the only playback controls. Theater mode and "Watch on Vimeo" stay. The custom play/seek/time/mute bar was removed; flipping the flag to `false` (a paid plan) means restoring that bar from git history.
- T18: `ChartExplore` (`src/components/sky/ChartExplore.tsx`, rendered by the home page) is active only while the journey rests on H3 with no panel open. Mouse drag pans within the chart bounds (40 px rubber band, inertia 0.94, none under reduced motion); ctrl/⌘ + wheel, trackpad pinch and two-finger touch zoom 1–2.5× about the pointer; one finger pans only once zoomed, so at 1× it still scrolls the page; the ± buttons and + − 0 and arrow keys (focus in the controls) do the same. Leaving H3 resets the view. Double-tap to zoom to a constellation is not built.
- Calm sky: off the home route (no panel target) and in the reduced-motion home below the hero, the scene fades constellation lines and content stars out and keeps the grid faint (`Scene.tsx`, `resolved.quiet`). Behind a panel (`resolved.bare`) the same happens except the focal star, which stays.
- `agentRules: false` in `next.config.ts` stops `next dev` from writing `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.
- OG: pages without a painting or still use `public/og/sky.jpg`, a 1200 × 630 render of the H3 chart. To regenerate it, screenshot the end of the home journey at 1200 × 630 with the header, footer and `#chart` hidden.
